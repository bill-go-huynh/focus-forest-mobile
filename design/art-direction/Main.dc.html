Focus Forest · Art direction sheet · draft 1

A quiet garden
at golden hour

A small, well-tended garden in late-afternoon light: warm, still, and slightly magical when the light catches something. The app is a place, not a tool panel.

Neutral ground

Paper, linen, bark and stone frame the app; night-moss in dark mode. The chrome recedes so the tree is the only living color on the screen.

Living color

Greens, blossoms, fruit and golden light belong to the tree scene and to growth. Green stays scarce in the interface, so it means something when it appears.

Warm light accent

Amber and sunlit gold, the color of low sun and lantern light. It marks Start Focus and moments of reward, and never competes with the green.

How the art is made

One key light, upper left. Highlights on the upper-left of every cluster, shadow pooled to the lower right.
Depth through value. Back layers lighter, hazier and less saturated; front layers carry the contrast.
Painterly-flat. Clean shapes, gentle gradients, a faint grain. Not corporate flat vector, not glossy 3D.
Layers, not pictures. Trunk, branches, back, main and front foliage, blossoms, fruit and traits stay separate.
